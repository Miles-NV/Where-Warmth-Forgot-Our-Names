# Important Decisions/Conversations
- **Survival Horror Aspects**  
	- Exhaustion for travel as the main driving focus
	- *Frequency is ok if its enjoyable to navigate situations, veiled with more interactive means to provide agency*
	- DM Comment: *Realism is not the focus* for the survival aspect.
- **Game Rules** (enemies favor from these as well, sans leveling)
	- We level this time we will take turns rolling hit die. I will roll one and you will roll one. Take the highest (reroll 1s).
	- Bonus action to take a potion yourself, action to use potion on someone else.
	- Flanking will give a +2 to hit and not advantage.
	- Critical Hits
		- Exploding Dice
			- **Critically Hit?** 
				- 1 x Guaranteed Max Damage Die, regardless of multi attack
				- Second Damage Die **may** explode | Max Damage without modifiers
			- **Max Damage Die?** (without modifiers)
				- Explodes into a new hit. 
	- At the beginning of each session I will hand out inspiration tokens to everyone. Feel free to use them anytime during the game.
- The way I run games:
    - I do NOT like the feel of DM vs Players. I will be rooting for you all to win. However, enemies will be smart and will do things that are advantageous to them and will hopefully challenge you a little.
    - As a result, player death is a thing that can happen. I won’t go out of my way to make it happen, but be aware that your character may die.
