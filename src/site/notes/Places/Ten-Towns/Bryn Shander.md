> 
>The defensive wall stands 10 meters high with two concentric rings of upright wooden poles, filled with dirt and rubble at the gap between them. The outer ring of poles rises above the wall, providing a rampart for defending militia on wood-planked walkways. The gates are 5m tall and can be fortified from the inside with iron-banded wood beams.
> 
>The gates are locked whenever darkness descends upon Bryn Shander, which has been most of the time in the past two years...

# Locations

## Armory

just off the central square, storing arms for the militia of Bryn Shander.

## Blackiron Blades

Manned by shield dwarf siblings, [[Places/Ten-Towns/Garn the Hammer\|Garn the Hammer]] and [[Elza\|Elza]], this *smithy and outfitter* provides both blade and supplies—including rations, cold weather gear, ice picks, and snowshoes—to fortune seekers. 

## House of the Morninglord

A converted home near the economic center of Shander, serving as a gathering hub for worshipers of [[Amaunator\|Amaunator]], god of the sun known to assume the role of both man and woman.

Overseen by [[Mishan\|Mishan]], adventurers can glimpse at the state of where the hearts of Shander find solace.

## The Northlook

The *go to* establishment for mercenaries and adventurers—with danger no less than mug of moonshine away. At the same time, the taproom is the best spot for leads on profitable ventures, rumors, and latest news.

## Townhall

Largest building bordering the central square. Reserved for community feasts and gatherings. Currently used to accommodate refugees from neighboring settlements.

## Geldenstag's Rest

The oldest inn in town. Run by widowed [[Myrtle\|Myrtle]], a known meddler of affairs, offering lackluster accommodations. Travelers seeking to stay at Geldenstag—wittingly or not— have dulled and expedited their stay in Bryn Shander. 

## Hooked Knucklehead

The longstanding inn of Bryn Shander. Welcoming traders, adventurers, vagabonds. Meager accommodations are to be expected at this establishment, with the common room bustling with clients huddled near the large stone hearth.   

## Kelvin's Comfort

The most popular tavern in town, stocked exclusively with dwarven ales and brandies, with a ill-tempered proprietor [[Ogden Flame-Beard\|Ogden Flame-Beard]]. 

## House of the Triad

Largest place of worship, the only true temple, carved of stone, built by dwarves of [[Kelvin's Cairn\|Kelvin's Cairn]]. Standing halfway between the southwest gate and the central market.

## Stables

Any horse, pony or mule can be quartered and fed for 1sp per night. 

## Speaker's Palace

A private residence, with an overstated moniker of "palace". Fashioned by dwarves, a far out of place dewlling amoung rough wood neighbors. [[Duvessa Shane\|Duvessa Shane]] is the current town speaker.

## Rendaril's Emporium

Largest trade house in Shander, on the site of the original cabin which started this settlement. The finest goods can be for steep prices.
