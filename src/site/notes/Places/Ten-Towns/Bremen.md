Founded by prospecting shield dwarves, this sleepy town sits on the west bank of [[Places/Ten-Towns/Maer Dualdon\|Maer Dualdon]], at the mouth of the [[Places/Ten-Towns/Shaengarne River\|Shaengarne River]]. Bremen's harbor is frozen due to the eternal winter, requiring local fishers to haul boasts across the ice to place them in the unfrozen sections. Visitors lacking a boat, must traverse the frozen segments of the [[Places/Ten-Towns/Shaengarne River\|Shaengarne River]].

# Locations

## Buried Treasures Inn

A fine establishment run by [[Characters/Ten-Towns/Bremen/Cora Mulphoon\|Cora Mulphoon]]. Due to the remote nature of Bremen, this inn does not get many visitors, if at all. Guests are treated with the most respect, as if they were royalty.

## Five-Tavern Center

At the center of Breme lies five taverns arranged in a semicircle around a central yard. As the story goes, five brothers originally intended to build a tavern together, but each had assumed to lead business operations. Since none of the brothers would cooperate and work of others, each built his own tavern, and they all compete for patrons.

The five taverns are 
- Stones
- Even Keel
- The River's Mouth
- The Grumpy Moose
- The Black-Bearded Brother. 

Due to fierce competitive business tactics, each tavern has been poorly maintained.
